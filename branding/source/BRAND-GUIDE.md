# DAYMARK Brand Files

Tagline: Plan your day. Track your progress.

## Included files
- `reference/DAYMARK-brand-guideline-sheet.png`: full brand guideline sheet generated in this conversation.
- `logo/DAYMARK-primary-light.png`: primary horizontal logo on light background.
- `logo/DAYMARK-primary-dark.png`: horizontal logo on dark background.
- `icons/DAYMARK-icon-transparent-1024.png`: extracted symbol on transparent background.
- `icons/DAYMARK-app-icon-1024.png`: square icon on warm off-white background.
- `icons/DAYMARK-icon-*.png`: common icon sizes.
- `android/`: Android launcher PNG sizes.
- `web/`: favicon PNG sizes and a sample PWA manifest.

## Palette
- Charcoal: `#202428`
- Gold: `#C9A85A`
- Warm off-white: `#F7F7F4`
- Secondary gray: `#6B7280`

## Integration notes
These are PNG exports/crops from the approved visual concept. The full sheet and lockups are raster images. The standalone icon is extracted from the generated sheet, so inspect its edge quality and transparency before shipping. For a truly editable vector logo, trace the approved symbol and wordmark in Inkscape or Illustrator and compare the vector to the reference.

Android adaptive icons may require separate foreground/background XML configuration in the Flutter Android project. The included PNGs are source assets, not a guarantee that the Android project is already configured.
